MetaPatch

NFC-based Semantic Interaction for User-defined Mixed Reality Experiences
Mixed RealityNear Field CommunicationTangible Interaction Wearable ComputingEveryday ObjectsAuthoring Tools
FieldHCI · Mixed Reality · Physical Computing
PlatformBody-worn NFC readers · BLE · OpenXR / Unity
Scope4 interaction primitives · 6 application scenarios

In most mixed reality systems, physical objects are static anchors whose meaning is defined by the system. MetaPatch treats cheap, battery-free NFC stickers as semantic interaction patches: rewritable tags that let anyone write meaning directly into an object, so it persists over time, accumulates across people, and travels with the object.

Touching a tagged object with a reader worn on the wrist, finger, or underfoot either reads back what has been written there, shown as an MR overlay anchored to the object, or starts a quick authoring flow. The system is framed by a three-layer interaction model: contextual (for whom, and when), operational (how contact behaves), and functional (what is shown, and where).

Skills Demonstrated

EMBEDDED SYSTEMS & WIRELESS

A PN532 NFC module and an Adafruit Feather nRF52840 bridge passive NTAG21x stickers to the headset across four protocol layers: 13.56 MHz RF, I²C, BLE 5.0 GATT, and OpenXR events.

Feather nRF52840 · PN532 · NTAG213/216 · NDEF ↔ JSON · BLE GATT

WEARABLE HARDWARE

Three reader placements (wrist band, finger panel, shoe insole), each with its own antenna geometry, read range, angle tolerance and debounce timing tuned to how the body makes contact.

Antenna sizing · TPU wristband · Insole integration · 10–32 ms tag reads

MIXED REALITY DEVELOPMENT

A Unity application on an OpenXR headset renders object-anchored overlays and uses hand tracking to turn the moment of touch into a spatial anchor, so anchoring and authoring happen in one act.

Unity · OpenXR · Hand tracking · Spatial anchors · Digital twins

TANGIBLE INTERFACE DESIGN & FABRICATION

3D-printed tangible proxies, spring-actuated buttons and tag-lined sliders on linear or circular tracks, give MR discrete and continuous input with no powered parts on the object.

PETG 3D printing · Button & slider proxies · Tag-pairing classifier

INTERACTION FRAMEWORK

A three-layer model of on-object semantic interaction that defines the design space and organises every primitive and scenario in the system.

Contextual · Operational · Functional layers

SCENARIO & EXPERIENCE DESIGN

Six applications across personal, shared and civic scales: furniture assembly, a physical card game with online play, gym equipment, a multiplayer game, resident-authored navigation, and street memory.

Personal → community → city scale
