3D Printing & Medical Visualization Intern

3DPP Lab, Duke-NUS Medical School / SingHealth
Lab3D Printing & Prototyping (3DPP) Lab
FocusMedical 3D Printing · Rapid Prototyping · Medical Visualization

Overview

Worked on the development of medical visualization solutions through additive manufacturing and rapid prototyping. Supported the design, fabrication, and evaluation of 3D-printed anatomical models for clinical education and surgical planning.

Working closely with doctors, resident doctors, and medical researchers, I supported healthcare innovation through medical visualization and rapid prototyping. My role included managing day-to-day lab operations, operating and maintaining specialized fabrication equipment, and supporting the development of training and clinical prototype solutions. I also helped medical educators and students build awareness of 3D visualization, medical 3D printing, and digital manufacturing through demonstrations, public lectures, and Open House activities.

Key Contributions

  • Developed and fabricated anatomical models using 3D printing workflows.
  • Prepared CAD models and optimized digital-to-physical fabrication pipelines.
  • Supported silicone molding and rapid prototyping processes for medical simulation devices.
  • Collaborated with clinicians and researchers to translate medical requirements into physical prototypes.
  • Assisted in laboratory operations, equipment management, and technical demonstrations.
